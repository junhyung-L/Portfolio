## Selected work

### Forestry crop suitability and yield analysis

[Forestry-Yield-Prediction](https://github.com/junhyung-L/Forestry-Yield-Prediction) connects GIS, climate, soil, and production data. I compared statistical checks and crop-specific classification/regression paths, then translated the analysis into a map-oriented cultivation decision concept. The work received the 2024 Grand Prize (Korea Forest Service Commissioner Award, first place).

### Dong-Incheon commercial-district marketing pilot

[O2O-Growth-Hacking](https://github.com/junhyung-L/O2O-Growth-Hacking) was a field research and store-marketing project. In the recorded Marketing & Data Analysis role, I worked with vacancy data and field findings, then supported a 30-day Madam Tea pilot across Naver Place, Instagram, and short-form content. It received the 2025 PBL Grand Prize (Incheon Metropolitan City Mayor Award, first place).

### Purchase conversion prediction with marketing-fatigue features

[Marketing-Fatigue-Analysis](https://github.com/junhyung-L/Marketing-Fatigue-Analysis) uses roughly 172M REES46 multichannel CRM logs to build purchase-cycle, response-timing, fatigue, and channel-effectiveness features. The manuscript reports domain-feature XGBoost F1 0.3806 and PR-AUC 0.3620 on its original-distribution holdout. Top-0.78% ranking captured 95.83% of conversions while selecting 99.22% fewer candidates; this is an offline ranking result, not realised savings.

### Incheon e-Eum cashback policy simulation

[eEum-Policy-Simulation](https://github.com/junhyung-L/eEum-Policy-Simulation) compares 66 cashback combinations under a budget constraint using regional-spending forecasts. It examines expected consumption, cashback outlay, and budget remaining together. The work received Gold at the 2025 Industry-Academic Capstone Design Competition; it is not causal policy-effect evidence.
